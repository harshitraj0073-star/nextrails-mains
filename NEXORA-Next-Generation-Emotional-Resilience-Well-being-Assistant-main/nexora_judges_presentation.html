Executive Brief & Technical Overview

NEXORA

Next-Generation Emotional Resilience & Distress-Monitoring Assistant for Victims in Legal & Rehabilitation Proceedings.

Role: Human-in-the-Loop Clinical AI Platform AI Model: Google Gemini 1.5/2.0 Flash UI: React 19 + Vite + Tailwind CSS Target: Legal, Police & Health Casework
🎯 1. Problem Statement & The NEXORA Solution

The Critical Problem

Victims of crime and trauma undergo protracted legal trials lasting months or years. Caseworkers handle 50+ cases simultaneously, resulting in infrequent check-ins. Emotional distress, anxiety, and suicidal ideation are usually detected reactively after an emergency occurs.

The NEXORA Innovation

NEXORA transforms victim support from reactive crisis control into proactive prevention. It provides continuous, empathetic AI check-ins, calculates clinical distress metrics, and alerts counsellors immediately when trauma thresholds are breached.

👥 2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Portals
Portal Role Core Functionality Impact & Clinical Value
Victim Portal Empathetic AI Companion chat, conversational stress analysis (High/Medium/Low), integrated 'Consult a Doctor' portal for instant specialist tele-consultations, and milestone tracking. Provides safe, judgment-free expression with no clinical stigma, available 24/7 in native languages.
Counsellor Portal Clinical triage dashboard, active distress telemetry (0–100), predictive trend warnings, and clinical notes management. Enables counsellors to prioritize high-risk victims immediately and conduct targeted interventions.
Authority / Admin Aggregated caseload analytics, resolution rates, caseworker workload distribution, and judicial phase tracking. Gives administrative leaders complete institutional oversight and optimal resource deployment.
🔄 3. End-to-End System Workflow
STEP 1 Victim Interaction Daily check-in / freeform chat with Gemini AI Companion
STEP 2 Distress Scoring Algorithm scores PHQ-9 / GAD-7 metrics (0–100)
STEP 3 Predictive Trigger Spike detected (>75) → automated high-priority alert
STEP 4 Human Intervention Counsellor reviews XAI insights and schedules outreach
⭐ 4. 10 Core Architectural & AI Capabilities
1Dynamic Distress & Stress Monitor
Continuous 0–100% scoring calculated from conversational sentiment, linguistic distress markers, and check-in surveys.
2Predictive Distress Engine
Trajectory forecasting using historical trend curves to identify crisis points before they occur.
3Multimodal Analysis
Multi-variable assessment synthesizing user sentiment, response frequency, and semantic markers.
4Adaptive Clinical Check-ins
Grounded in clinical psychology standards (PHQ-9 for depression, GAD-7 for generalized anxiety).
5AI Companion & Concluding Stress Assessment
Google Gemini-powered companion that evaluates conversational distress and provides an instant 0–100% stress breakdown with emotional tone telemetry on session conclusion.
6Crisis & Threat Detection
Instant detection of self-harm or critical triggers with automatic emergency hotline injection (Tele-MANAS, 1091).
7Smart Alert Escalation Matrix
Automated tiering (Low, Moderate, High, Critical) ensuring prompt notification to caseworkers.
8Human-in-the-Loop Interventions
AI recommends; human experts decide. Caseworkers log notes, resolve alerts, and schedule sessions.
9Victim Case Journey Tracker
Visual legal & recovery milestone tracker (FIR, Court Hearings, Therapy, Rehabilitation).
10Explainable AI & Privacy (XAI)
Transparent rationales detailing why scores altered, maintaining ethical and clinical compliance.
